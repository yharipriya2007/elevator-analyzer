Problem Description

  • Elevator cabin connected using a pulley and cable system.
  • Mass of elevator is considered as the main load.
  • Pulley assumed smooth and massless.
  • Motor provides torque to lift the elevator.
  • String/cable assumed inextensible.

Objectives

  • Determine cable tension.
  • Calculate motor force and torque.
  • Analyze elevator motion using Newton’s Second Law.
  • Study effect of acceleration on lifting force.
