Objectives

  • To design a working model of an elevator using pulley mechanism.
  • To calculate pulley tension and lifting force.
  • To analyze motor torque required to lift the cabin.
  • To determine cabin speed and travel time.
  • To check safety factor of rope and motor suitability.
  • To understand practical applications of Engineering Mechanics.

Model Specifications

  • Shaft Height = 60 cm
  • Cabin Height = 10 cm
  • Cabin Width = 8 cm
  • Pulley Diameter = 5 cm
  • Motor Speed = 100 rpm
  • Motor Voltage = 6V
  • Rope Type = Nylon Thread
